No. 03 Data capture

WearStream

A self-hosted container that keeps a complete local copy of your raw Whoop and Garmin Connect data. On a schedule it writes exactly what the vendors returned into an append-only tree, the raw layer of your own data lake.

  • Re-captures recent days on every run, because vendors revise them, and keeps only what changed
  • Fills in older history one day at a time, well inside the vendors’ rate limits
  • Signed image with an SBOM and build provenance; runs as non-root on a read-only filesystem
Stack
Python · uv · Chainguard distroless
Runs as
Single Docker container
